# PeerJ form draft

## Title

scReg-Eval: a fixed-panel audit of regulatory alignment in single-cell RNA foundation-model gene graphs

## Short title

Fixed-panel audit of scFM gene graphs

## Article scope

The study audits whether single-cell RNA foundation-model gene graphs align with a sequence- and
accessibility-derived regulatory-potential proxy after controlling co-expression and structural
covariates. It reports finite-panel randomization inference, descriptive cell-type analyses, and a
pipeline-sensitivity diagnostic. The proxy is not causal regulatory ground truth.

## Subject areas

Bioinformatics; Computational Biology; Artificial Intelligence; Data Science.

## Suggested keywords

single-cell RNA-seq; foundation models; gene regulatory networks; co-expression confounding;
randomization inference; benchmark audit

## Data availability

Primary datasets are public: GSE174367 (brain snATAC-seq), 10x Genomics 10k PBMC multiome, and
GSE206767 (fibroblast ATAC). Code repository: https://github.com/PeterPonyu/scfm-reg-audit
Archive DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21724336
