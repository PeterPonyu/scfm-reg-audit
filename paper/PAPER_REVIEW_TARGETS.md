# Paper review targets

This directory holds figure-generation sources for scReg-Eval. It is separate from
`research/sc-fm-benchmark`, which is a different paper.

## Human review target (public tree)

- `figs/` — TikZ fragments regenerated by `make_figs.R`.
- `submission_peerj/flat_upload/` — figure PDFs `Figure1.pdf` (study design) through
  `Figure13.pdf` (coverage QC) plus `FigureA1.pdf`–`FigureA3.pdf`. Checksums:
  `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Full manuscript TeX/PDF sources are local-only and are not on the public repository HEAD.
