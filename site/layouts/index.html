Fixed-panel audit

scReg-Eval

scRNA foundation-model gene graphs, scored on a frozen 446 TF × 1,200 gene panel against a two-tissue regulatory-potential proxy. The object is the audit.

  • Panel 446 TFs × 1,200 genes Brain snATAC (GSE174367) + 10x PBMC multiome. Fibroblast mix (GSE206767) is construct-only.
  • Dual-null Support iff both q < 0.05 Monte Carlo gene-label (Mantel-style) and degree-preserving shuffle, N=999. Support ≠ protocol-pass ≠ recovery.
  • Protocol-pass 0/13 Predeclared conjunction. Frozen. Support under the full spec is not a pass.

The object

On the frozen panel, is partial-Spearman alignment between a frozen foundation-model gene graph and the accessibility/motif proxy unusually large under a named confound design and two named Monte Carlo randomizations, after within-family BH?

  • Support Both Monte Carlo qM and degree-preserving qD < 0.05 in the same confound-spec. Panel-local unusualness only.
  • Protocol-pass Stricter conjunction on each of 13 FM/readout rows: dual-full ∧ concordance ∧ non-degree same sign ∧ multi-readout sign ∧ ρ above the co-expression baseline. Freeze: 0/13.
  • Recovery Causal TF–target recovery is not the estimand. Dual-null Support without protocol-pass is residual geometry under a disclosed degree hazard — not recovery.

Construct

The proxy is sequence-derived: TF motif matches in accessible peaks, gated by per-cell-type accessibility. It does not use RNA covariation, so a graph that only encodes “genes that move together” cannot satisfy it by construction.

  • Tissues Two-tissue proxy Brain snATAC and paired PBMC multiome are the audit tissues. The fibroblast mix is a construct-lane / transfer check, not a third scored tissue.
  • Rows 13 FM / readout rows Geneformer, scGPT, scFoundation, UCE. CellPLM is out of scope: no per-gene contextual state after pooling.
